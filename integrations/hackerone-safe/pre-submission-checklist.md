# Pre-Submission Checklist

## Authorization

- [ ] The program policy was reviewed recently.
- [ ] The exact asset is listed as in scope.
- [ ] The exact technique is allowed.
- [ ] Rate limits and testing windows were followed.
- [ ] No third-party or excluded asset was tested.
- [ ] Ambiguities were resolved through the program's official channel.

## Safety

- [ ] Testing was non-destructive and stopped at the minimum proof.
- [ ] No persistence, malware, denial of service, spam, or social engineering
      was used.
- [ ] No unrelated accounts or data were accessed.
- [ ] Any sensitive data encountered was not copied or retained.
- [ ] Local evidence is stored outside the repository and is access-controlled.

## Report quality

- [ ] The title states the affected asset and security impact.
- [ ] Steps are complete, concise, and reproducible.
- [ ] Expected and actual behavior are clearly separated.
- [ ] Impact is concrete and not overstated.
- [ ] Evidence is minimal and fully redacted.
- [ ] Credentials, tokens, cookies, personal data, and secrets are absent.
- [ ] The severity rationale matches the program's taxonomy.
- [ ] Remediation addresses the root cause.

## Final gate

- [ ] A human reviewed the complete report.
- [ ] AI-generated claims were independently verified.
- [ ] The report is being submitted only through HackerOne.
- [ ] The report ID and submission timestamp will be recorded.
